FUTURE MISSION DATABASE

Collect now, publish when the structure is stable

The raw samples can become a searchable mission database without pretending unfinished PTS patterns are final.

01

Capture a complete record

Mission name, level, stars, PvE/PvP, waves, duration, distance, scan and claim costs, reward ranges, enemy fleets, combat techs, class, universe settings, and evidence source.

02

Normalize and compare

Keep repeated samples under the same mission template, retain the original values, and flag changed mechanics by PTS version instead of overwriting history.

03

Publish when useful

Add filters and comparisons after the schema survives PTS changes and there are enough repeated samples to separate patterns from one-off rolls.

!What can be filled now?

Only fields supported by a complete screenshot, direct test, or official post. Layout-only screenshots do not close mission-data gaps, and incomplete records stay out of the public database.
