EVIDENCE POLICY

Useful without pretending certainty

Official statements, direct PTS observations, confirmed formulas, and working estimates are labeled separately. Missing values are left unknown rather than filled with invented numbers.

Raw screenshots containing account state, coordinates, or other private details are evidence sources—not public website assets.
