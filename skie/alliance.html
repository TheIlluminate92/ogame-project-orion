How to use this manual

Follow the numbered learning path if you are building foundations. For a specific question, search the library or jump to a chapter inside a lesson. Each guide explains an action, gives a practical example, and finishes with checks you can perform in your own account.

Useful knowledge needs context

Strategies depend on your universe, account, class, available time, and risk tolerance. Examples are not a mandatory build order. Historical Orion research is deliberately kept in its own archive, with later changes and validation gaps visible.

Private by design

This static website has no account login, analytics, message sender, or game connection. Optional reading progress is stored only in your browser. Copying or downloading a lesson does not send it anywhere automatically.
